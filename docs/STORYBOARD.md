## Global experience

- Open on a full-screen editorial cover.
- Provide a guided “Begin” path and persistent chapter navigation for free exploration.
- Use book-like transitions, layered page compositions, depth and parallax inspired by the reference's interaction quality.
- Use motion to reveal and connect content, not to obstruct reading.
- Keep essential copy as accessible HTML; use WebGL/canvas for decorative depth and visual effects where appropriate.
- Sound is opt-in only, starts muted, and has an obvious control.
- Support mouse, touch, keyboard navigation and reduced-motion preferences.
- On smaller screens, simplify layered effects while preserving the editorial hierarchy and content.
- The original logo and portraits are source assets. Do not redraw, replace, distort or generate substitutes for them.

## Visual system guardrails

- Existing brand profile includes gold/orange accent in some supplied social creatives, while the earlier book direction specified red. Treat red as the current book direction, but confirm final accent treatment against the official brand assets before implementation; do not recolour the logo.
- Use high-contrast editorial typography, generous negative space and deliberate image crops.
- The supplied portraits are the actual team assets. No stock or generated replacements.
- Use the official logo without modifying its geometry.
- Avoid excessive decorative effects that reduce legibility or mobile performance.

## Pre-build content dependencies

1. Verify the exact original logo file and transparency/background behaviour.
2. Verify portrait-to-member mapping and select the highest-resolution originals.
3. Collect approved portfolio images, project labels, member attribution and display permissions.
4. Confirm the book's final accent colour treatment (red versus gold/orange accents visible in supplied social creatives).
5. Review and approve the final manuscript and storyboard together before application code begins.
